Position Monitor — Quick Manual

An 8×8 currency matrix — read and bulk-manage every open position across the 28 majors, at a glance.
IN CHART VERITAS ⚡

01What it is

The Position Monitor is a portfolio cockpit: an on-chart 8×8 grid that shows all your open positions across the 28 major pairs and the 8 currency baskets in one place, plus one-click bulk actions to reduce or close the whole book.

It complements AutoQuickTrade: AutoQuickTrade opens and manages positions per pair / basket, the Monitor gives you the overview and the account-wide controls. It reads the account — it does not open new trades.

02The 8×8 matrix

Lower triangle — pairs

The 28 cross cells are the 28 major pairs. Each cell aggregates all open positions on that pair (all pyramid layers, both directions) into one net figure.

Diagonal — baskets

The 8 diagonal cells are the currency baskets (#EUR … #JPY). B:n shows how many positions belong to that currency's basket.

03Reading a cell

  • Fill colour — P/L sign: green = in profit, red = in loss
  • Value — net pips or net money (toggled by Show pips)
  • "S" marker — at least one leg on that pair has no stop-loss (naked)
  • Blue / magenta frame — a pending order is armed (blue = BUY, magenta = SELL)
  • Teal frame — the pair is secured at net break-even (see next section)

04The net break-even frame

A teal ring around a pair cell means its stack is secured at or above net break-even: if every leg's stop-loss were hit right now, the sum across all legs would net ≥ 0 (after estimated commission and swap).

Aggregated, not per-leg

Judged over all legs together. Example: three EURUSD layers at +10 / +16 / −20 pips — the frame looks at the sum, so the winners can carry the loser and the cell still counts as secured.

What blocks the frame

A naked leg (the "S" marker) → never framed (open downside). A pending frame takes precedence over the teal one on the same cell.

Most accurate with AutoQuickTrade. The check subtracts the commission the Monitor learned from your fills, so it is an estimate. It is most precise when the stop was placed by AutoQuickTrade's SECURE (net-BE mode), which sets the SL exactly on the cost-inclusive net break-even using the same cost model. A manually-set stop is judged only as well as the learned commission — and without a learned rate the frame effectively means raw break-even + swap.
"Secured" = no loss at the SL, not a guarantee. Gaps and slippage can still slip past a stop. The frame is a live estimate for the overview — stops are placed from AutoQuickTrade, not from the Monitor.

05Status strip & net P/L

The line under the matrix summarises the book:

7 positions | +27.8p | net: +56.31 (c:12.82)

  • net — profit + swap − estimated round-trip commission (shown as c:)
  • counts of no-SL and pending appear only when > 0, to keep the strip clean

06Footer & bulk actions

The footer carries the account figures and the one-click controls (all across the current Magic filter):

HIDE privacy toggle   ALLPROFITLOSS close all / profitable / losing   -25%-50% bulk partial close

Two-click confirm. Every bulk button (ALL / PROFIT / LOSS / −25% / −50%) needs a second click within Confirmation timeout seconds — the button shows CONFIRM? after the first click. The partial close is lot-safe: a position is skipped if the closed part or the remainder would fall below the broker minimum lot.

07Privacy (HIDE)

HIDE replaces the money values (balance, equity, delta, and the net figure) with ***. Pips stay visible. Ideal for screenshots and streaming without exposing account size.

08Magic filter

Magic Number filter scopes the whole Monitor — matrix, net total and bulk actions — to one strategy:

  • 0 — all positions on the account
  • a magic — only positions with that magic (e.g. one basket that AutoQuickTrade stamped)

09Click to chart

Click any cell to open (or focus) that symbol's chart on the Timeframe to open on click, using your Chart template if set. Jump straight from the overview to the pair you want to manage in AutoQuickTrade.

11Notes & safety

  • Hedging account — matches AutoQuickTrade, which manages each pyramid layer as its own ticket
  • Bulk actions are account-wide within the magic filter — the two-click confirm is your safeguard; read the button before the second click
  • The net figure, the c: commission and the net-BE frame are estimates (learned commission), not the broker's exact settlement
  • The Test build is a time-limited beta; the marketplace build has no expiry
  • Educational / analysis tool — not financial advice

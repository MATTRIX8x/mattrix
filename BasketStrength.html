Basket Strength

Reads one basket at a glance: how far it has moved, how many of its legs agree, and which leg leads.
IN CHART VERITAS

01What it is

A panel in the top-right of a MATTRIX synthetic chart. It breaks the basket into its individual legs, sorts them strongest-first, and answers the question a single price chart cannot: is this move broad, or is it one pair doing all the work?

Panel

  • Basket move (big number) + unit
  • Coherence — how many legs agree
  • Signal label (CHOPPY → MIXED → STRONG → EXTREME)
  • Every leg with arrow and value, sorted

On the chart

  • CLS lines: entry, SL and TP of the running basket
  • Click any leg name to open that pair’s chart

02Setup

  • Drop it on a MATTRIX synthetic chart (#EUR, #USD, #RON, #MyMag7 …). On a normal broker pair it will report WRONG CHART and stop.
  • The SyntheticCharts EA must have created the # symbols first.
  • Set Broker suffix if your FX symbols carry one (e.g. .a).

03Which baskets it reads

ChartLegsUnit
#EUR … #JPY7 pairspips
#RON / #ROFF8 pairspips
Any other # chartup to 8 memberspips or %

Custom baskets need no configuration. The members are read from the synthetic symbol’s own description — the list SyntheticCharts writes there (AMD+CSCO+CVX+JNJ+…). Free text in the description is ignored, so a prefix like MyTop8: does no harm. Change the synthetic and the panel follows; there is no list to keep in sync.

The unit picks itself. If every member is an FX pair, the panel measures in pips — the familiar behaviour. As soon as one member is not FX (a stock, index or metal), it switches to percent, because a $1 move means something entirely different on a $170 share than on a $610 one. Percent legs are measured against the previous close, the way any quote screen does it, so gaps are counted properly.

04Reading the panel

The big number is the basket move for the current bar. For a currency basket it is the summed pip move of all legs; for a custom basket it is taken from the synthetic series itself, so it reflects exactly the weighting your basket is built with.

Coherence is the count that matters: 6/7 means six of seven legs point the same way. It is the difference between a currency being bought across the board and a single cross running away.

SignalMeaning
CHOPPYBelow ~60% agreement — no usable direction.
MIXED ▲/▼Majority agrees, but the basket is not aligned.
STRONG ▲/▼~85% or more of the legs agree — this is breadth.
EXTREME ▲/▼STRONG plus momentum beyond the extreme threshold. Powerful, but also where moves exhaust.

The leg list is sorted strongest first, so the leader and the laggard are always at the ends. Click any leg name to open that pair’s chart in the timeframe and template you configured.

Watch for divergence. When the arrow disagrees with the number — the basket is net positive but most legs point down — the move is carried by a few strong crosses, not by breadth. Coherence will show it as a low count. Treat that as a weaker signal, not a trend.

05CLS lines

If the MATTRIX EA is running a basket with the same EA Magic Number, the panel draws its entry, stop and target onto the synthetic chart, so you see the running trade in basket terms rather than per pair. Drag a line and the update pauses briefly so it does not fight you.

07How to use it

  • Pick the basket, not the pair. Compare coherence across your # charts and trade the one with real breadth behind it.
  • Confirm, never trigger. The panel tells you how broad a move is. Where and when to act stays a chart decision.
  • Watch the extremes. EXTREME means the move is powerful and stretched at the same time — a level to respect in both directions.
Correlated baskets. Several baskets showing STRONG in the same direction are usually one trade seen from different sides — most FX moves are a dollar move. Breadth is a signal; it is not diversification.
